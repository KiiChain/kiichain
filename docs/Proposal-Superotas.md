# Superotas Proposal

## Overview
**Superotas** is a decentralized application (dApp) built on **Kiichain (Cosmos SDK)** and powered by **Chainlink** for real-time data aggregation.
The app aggregates and compares supermarket prices, making grocery shopping more efficient, transparent, and competitive.

## Core Concept
- Supermarkets register their products, units, and prices (retail and wholesale) in a software.
- Software manages stock, promotions, expiration dates, and competitor price variations.
- **Chainlink oracles** feed real-time prices into the blockchain.
- **Superotas app** consumes this data, offering users a seamless and trustworthy shopping experience.

## Features for Users
- Real-time price quotations for daily, weekly, or monthly shopping.
- Easy comparison between different supermarkets in the same region.
- Personalized and highly customizable experience.

## Business Model
- Supermarkets subscribe at a cost-effective price.
- Daily “basket of most-searched products” is generated.
- Supermarket with best pricing wins daily competition.
- **5% of daily app revenue** rewarded as vouchers to the top-spending user.
- Vouchers redeemable only at winning supermarket.

## Benefits
- Users: save money and shop smarter.
- Supermarkets: increase competitiveness and loyalty.
- Blockchain: real-world adoption of **Chainlink** + **Kiichain**.
